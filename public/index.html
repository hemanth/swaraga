Swaraga combines 16 kHz microtonal pitch DSP with 962 catalogued ragas + open-set Melakarta/Janya combinatorics and 10 parallel TypeSafe System One (choice, score, noul) primitives. 01 ACOUSTIC DSP & YIN F0 SCIENCE

Shadja Lock & 1200¢ Microtonal Tracking

Decodes vocal audio at 16 kHz via Web Audio API and estimates fundamental frequency F0 using the YIN cumulative mean normalized difference function d'(τ) = d(τ) / ((1/τ)∑d(j)) with parabolic interpolation. Locks tonic Shadja (Sa) via fifth/fourth (Pa/Ma) harmonic salience and maps every frame to c = 1200 log₂(f₀ / f_Sa) mod 1200, smoothed with a 7-point median filter and a >350¢ consonant plosive despiker.

02 RAGA COMBINATORICS (962 + 34,848+)

Why 962 Named Ragas + Open-Set Synthesis?

Roughly 300–500 ragas are actively performed in Hindustani and Carnatic concerts, while classical compendia (Sangita Sampradaya Pradarshini, Raga Nidhi) document ~1,000 named ragas. Mathematically, Venkatamakhin's 72 Melakartas × 484 straight 5/6/7-note (Audava / Shadava / Sampurna) Aroha × Avaroha subsets yield 34,848 Upanga Janya scales—plus infinite Vakra and Bhashanga phrasing ("Ananta vai ragah").

03 TWO-STAGE FULL-CATALOG MATCHER

962-Raga Lexicon + 13th Open-Set Hypothesis

Swaraga indexes 962 named ragas (72 Melakartas, 807 Carnatic Janyas, 61 Hindustani Thaat ragas, and 22 Dual Hindustani–Carnatic pairs). Stage 1 scores all 962 ragas via 0.42·Recall − 1.15·VarjyaLeak + 0.32·Jaccard + 0.28·Pakad + VadiBonus to shortlist the top 12 candidates, plus a 13th out_of_catalog hypothesis that synthesizes the exact Janya Jati, parent Melakarta, and nearest Thaat for any unlisted scale.

04 SYSTEM ONE & ZERO-KEY WASM

10 Typed Primitives & Hierarchical Beam

Compiles extracted swara telemetry into 10 batched System One primitives: 4× choice() (Primary Raga, Parent Thaat, Idiom, Prahar), 2× score() (Gamaka & Rasa 0–3 rubrics), and 4× noul() (Pakad & Vadi verifiers). Candidates are ranked by geometric beam √(P(Thaat) × P(Raga)) via TypeSafe Cloud (jev-latest) or in-browser OpenJev (@wllama/wllama Qwen3-0.6B 1-token direct option logit readout).

01 TONIC INVARIANCE ABLATION (Δ = −28.8%)

Why Raw Audio Embeddings Break Under Key Shifts

Gemma4AudioFeatureExtractor tokenizes 16 kHz audio via 128-bin HTK mel-spectrograms (0–8,000 Hz, 20 ms frame, 10 ms hop, 25 tokens/s). In Indian classical music, a raga is defined by relative frequency ratios to Adhara Shadja (Sa), not absolute pitch. Shifting tonic by a tritone (D3 = 146.8 Hz → G#3 = 207.7 Hz) translates every harmonic by √2 across mel bins, dropping EmbeddingGemma 2 Audio k-NN from 75.0% → 46.2% (−28.8%), whereas YIN circular cent folding 1200 log₂(f₀/f_Sa) mod 1200 retains 100% → 88.5%.

02 THE BI-ENCODER NEGATION PARADOX

Why "Omits M2 & N3" Attracts M2 & N3 Queries

In a dense 768-d mean-pooled bi-encoder, listing forbidden Varjya swaras in a raga's text card (e.g., "Raga Bhupali omits Teevra Madhyam M2 and Shuddha Nishad N3") injects positive token embeddings for M2 and N3 into Bhupali's vector—causing Bhupali (0.8516) to outscore Yaman (0.8487) on heptatonic Yaman queries. Removing negated Varjya text and encoding only positive spelled-out swaras lifts Yaman to #1 (0.8784), proving why Swaraga enforces an algebraic −1.15 × VarjyaLeakage penalty.

03 MATRYOSHKA MRL & L2 RENORMALIZATION

768d → 512d Lossless · 256d (1:3) · 128d (1:6)

EmbeddingGemma 2 is trained with Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL). Slicing a unit vector v[0:d] breaks unit norm (‖v[0:d]‖₂ < 1), so L2 renormalization v̂ = v[0:d] / ‖v[0:d]‖₂ is mandatory before dot-product scoring. Empirically, 512d (1:1.5 compression) is 100% lossless on the 14-raga pool (47.8% Top-1, 73.9% Top-3, 0.626 MRR), 256d (1:3) retains 67.4% Top-3 recall (91.2% retention), and 128d drops to 56.5% Top-3.

04 HONEST ARCHITECTURAL TIERING

Bi-Encoder Pruner (73.9% Top-3) vs. System One Executor (93.5%)

Shared-swara sibling ragas—Bhupali vs. Deshkar (both S R2 G3 P D2, Vadi G3 vs. D2) and Miyan ki Todi vs. Multani (both S r1 g2 M2 P d1 N3)—score 40.0%–53.3% in EmbeddingGemma 2 because single-vector pooling compresses sequential phrase order and dwell hierarchy. By pairing Stage 1 deterministic pruning with Stage 2 TypeSafe System One (choice, score, noul), Swaraga resolves sibling pairs at 93.3% Top-1 in 367 ms.
