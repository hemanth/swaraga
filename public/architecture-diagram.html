How Swaraga Works

End-to-end signal and decision topology from raw vocal audio to calibrated Hindustani and Carnatic raga judgments.

01 ACOUSTIC DSP & YIN F0 SCIENCE

Shadja-Locked F0 & 1200¢ Trajectory

  • Decodes vocal audio at 16 kHz via Web Audio API and computes the YIN cumulative mean normalized difference function d'(τ) = d(τ) / ((1/τ)∑d(j)) with parabolic interpolation.
  • Locks tonic Sa via harmonic fifth/fourth (Pa/Ma) pitch-class salience and maps frames to c = 1200 log₂(f₀ / f_Sa) mod 1200.
  • Applies a 7-point median filter and >350¢ transient consonant despiker to segment continuous Svara-Lipi note ribbons.

02 RAGA COMBINATORICS (962 NAMED + 34,848+ OPEN-SET)

Concert Repertoire vs. Mathematical Scales

  • Concert vs. Compendia: ~300–500 ragas are actively performed in concerts; classical encyclopedias (Sangita Sampradaya Pradarshini, Raga Nidhi) document ~1,000 named ragas.
  • 34,848 Upanga Janya Scales: Venkatamakhin's 72 Melakartas × 484 straight 5/6/7-note (Audava / Shadava / Sampurna) Aroha × Avaroha subsets yield 72 × 484 = 34,848 scales, plus open-ended Vakra & Bhashanga phrasing ("Ananta vai ragah").

03 TWO-STAGE FULL-CATALOG & OPEN-SET MATCHER

962 Catalogued Ragas + 13th Janya Hypothesis

  • 962 Named Ragas: Indexes 72 Melakartas, 807 Carnatic Janyas, 61 Hindustani Thaat ragas, and 22 Dual Hindustani–Carnatic pairs.
  • Stage 1 Shortlist: Scores all 962 ragas via 0.42·Recall − 1.15·VarjyaLeak + 0.32·Jaccard + 0.28·Pakad + VadiBonus to forward the top 12 candidates.
  • Stage 2 Open-Set (out_of_catalog): Adds a 13th hypothesis that synthesizes the exact Janya Jati, parent Melakarta, and nearest Thaat for any uncatalogued scale.

04 SYSTEM ONE PRIMITIVES & DUAL RUNTIME

10 Batched Judgments & Zero-Key Browser WASM

  • 10 Primitives: 4× choice() (Primary Raga, Parent Thaat, Idiom, Prahar), 2× score() (Gamaka & Rasa 0–3 rubrics), and 4× noul() (Pakad & Vadi verifiers).
  • Hierarchical Beam: Ranks candidates by geometric beam √(P(Thaat) × P(Raga)).
  • Cloud BYOK or Local WASM: Evaluates via POST /v1/systemone (jev-latest) or auto-loads OpenJev (@wllama/wllama Qwen3-0.6B) in-browser when no API key is present.
