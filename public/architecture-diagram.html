How Swaraga Works

End-to-end signal and decision topology from raw vocal audio to calibrated Hindustani and Carnatic raga judgments.

01 SIGNAL EXTRACTION

Shadja-Locked F0 & Swara Telemetry

  • Decodes vocal audio at 22.05 kHz and locks tonic Sa via harmonic pitch-class saliences or manual override.
  • Runs YIN/NSDF autocorrelation with a 7-point median filter and transient consonant despiker to trace microtonal 0–1200¢ trajectories.
  • Extracts 12-Swara dwell shares, Vadi/Samvadi candidates, Arohana/Avarohana runs, and top bigram transitions.

02 TYPESAFE PRIMITIVES

10 Batched System One Calls

  • 2× choice(): Calibrated probability distributions over 9 parent Thaats and top-6 candidate Ragas.
  • 4× score(): Continuous [0,1] rubrics evaluating Arohana/Avarohana adherence, Pakad n-gram fidelity, Gamaka/Meend ornamentation, and Rasa alignment.
  • 4× noul(): Ternary yes / no / uncertain verifiers checking Vadi dominance, Varjya swara avoidance, and Carnatic Melakarta equivalence.

03 DUAL RUNTIME & GATE

Cloud API, OpenJev WASM & Arbiter

  • TypeSafe Cloud: Single batched HTTP call to POST /v1/systemone with BYOK browser key or server .env key.
  • OpenJev Local: Zero-server in-browser GGUF inference via @wllama/wllama (`Qwen3-0.6B`, `MiniCPM-2B`, `Qwen3.5-4B`).
  • Routing Gate: Computes √(P(Thaat) × P(Raga)) and harmonic mean confidence to emit AUTO_LOCK_RAGA vs CANDIDATE_ENSEMBLE.
